MedMap Project

Description: MedMap visualizes the current hospital network, 
identifies areas with poor access, and recommends where an 
additional hospital could produce the greatest improvement 
in access.

Explanation: We don't simply choose the place with the fewest hospitals. 
For every potential location, MedMap simulates adding a hospital and 
calculates how much that location would reduce population-weighted 
travel distance to care.
